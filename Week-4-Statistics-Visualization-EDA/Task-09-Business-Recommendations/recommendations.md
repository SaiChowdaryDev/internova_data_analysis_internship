# Task 9 – Business Recommendations

## Recommendations

### 1. Encourage Consistent Study Time

The analysis shows a positive relationship between study hours and marks. Students who spend more time studying generally tend to achieve better marks.

**Recommendation:**  
Educational institutions can encourage students to follow a regular study schedule and provide additional study resources for students who need support.

### 2. Monitor Student Attendance

The analysis shows a positive relationship between attendance and marks. Students with better attendance generally show better academic performance in this dataset.

**Recommendation:**  
Institutions can monitor attendance regularly and identify students with low attendance early so that appropriate support can be provided.

## Conclusion

The analysis of the student dataset helped identify relationships between study hours, attendance, and academic performance. These findings can be used to support students through better study planning and attendance monitoring.
