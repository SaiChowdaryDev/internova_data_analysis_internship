# Task 7 - Business Insights & Recommendations

## Business Insights

1. Electronics generated the highest sales among the categories.
2. North region recorded strong overall sales performance.
3. Sales increased during some later months, showing periods of stronger demand.
4. Higher-value orders contributed significantly to total revenue.
5. Stationery had lower sales compared with Electronics and Furniture.

## Recommendations

1. Focus more on high-performing Electronics products and maintain sufficient stock to meet customer demand.
2. Analyze the lower-performing Stationery category and consider promotions or discounts to improve sales.

## Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard helped identify important sales trends, category performance and regional patterns. These insights can support better inventory planning and sales decisions.
